# Interior & Architectural Design – Online Reference Resources

Planning a renovation or building project? This curated collection maps free, high-quality online resources to essential interior design topics: room dimensions, clearances, kitchen planning, bathroom layouts, exteriors, and building services. The focus is on European and metric standards, with a dedicated section for German resources.

## Layout Rules & Dimensions

### Reference Books (Industry Standards)

- **Neufert – Bauentwurfslehre / Architects' Data** –
  The definitive German reference for architectural planning. Contains
  comprehensive metric data on room sizes, clearances, and human dimensions.
  Based on DIN standards. 44th German edition (2024) available.
- **Metric Handbook (Pamela Buxton)** –
  [PDF](The_Metric_Handbook_Architecture_must_have.pdf) |
  UK equivalent to Neufert. Covers all building types with metric planning data.

### German Building Regulations

- **Wohnflächenverordnung (WoFlV)** – German living space calculation standard.
  Key rules: rooms under 1m height = 0%, 1–2m = 50%, balconies = 25–50%.
- **DIN 277** – Alternative calculation method counting all areas at 100%.
- **Landesbauordnungen** – State building codes define minimum room sizes
  (varies by Bundesland).

### Renovation Planning Phases

1. **Pre-Planning (6–12 months before)**

   - Define objectives and must-haves
   - Research local zoning and permits
   - Set preliminary budget

2. **Design Phase**

   - Create detailed plans
   - Finalize material selections
   - Get contractor quotes

3. **Execution**

   - Manage timeline and milestones
   - Track budget and change orders
   - Quality checkpoints

4. **Completion**
   - Final inspections
   - Punch list items
   - Documentation
